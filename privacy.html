Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026.

This policy covers rclone WRK, a private OAuth client for the open-source rclone tool. It is used only by its owner, and only with the owner's own Google accounts.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, rclone WRK requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). This lets rclone list, read, create, update and delete files in the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used

The data is used only to make the owner's Drive files available as a folder on the owner's own server and to keep that folder in sync with Google Drive. rclone WRK does not use the data for advertising, profiling or analytics, does not sell it, and does not use it to train models.

Storage

OAuth tokens and cached copies of files are stored only on the owner's server, in files readable only by the owner's account.

Sharing

rclone WRK does not share Google user data with any third party. Files made available on the owner's server are used by the owner with other software of the owner's choosing.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account page for third-party connections, myaccount.google.com/connections. After access is revoked, rclone WRK can no longer reach Google Drive.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page.
